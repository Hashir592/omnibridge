# Runbook: support answers

Short answers for the `support@` mailbox. Add a line when the same question comes in twice.

## Sign-in

- **"My account is locked" / "I keep getting locked out"** (USR-5): 10 wrong attempts lock the email for 15 minutes, and anyone who knows the email can cause it, so a repeated lock may be someone else guessing. The answer is to wait 15 minutes or reset the password by the emailed link; support cannot unlock it early. At most one lock email reaches the user per day (S5-20261117-03), so a missing second email is expected.
