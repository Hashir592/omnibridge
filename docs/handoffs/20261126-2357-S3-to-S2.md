The routes for your screen (all session routes, Connections tab, admins only, company-scoped):
- `POST /connections/website` (`createWebsiteConnection`) takes `company_id`, `display_name`, `base_url` (https only) and `outbound_api_key` (16 to 512 characters). It answers 201 with `connection` (the usual Connection row), `settings` and `keys`.
  - `keys.inbound_api_key` and `keys.signing_secret` are in this answer only. Show them once, with copy buttons and a plain line saying they cannot be shown again.
- `GET /connections/{accountId}/website` (`getWebsiteSettings`) answers:
  - `connection_id` and `inbound_url`: the website's developers need both;
  - `base_url`;
  - `inbound_key_hint` (last 4 characters only);
  - `keys_issued_at`;
  - `outbound_key_set` (never the key).
- `PUT /connections/{accountId}/website` (`updateWebsiteSettings`) changes `base_url`, `outbound_api_key` or both. Sending neither is 400.
- `POST /connections/{accountId}/website/keys` (`reissueWebsiteKeys`) makes a new key and secret, shown once. The old ones stop working at once, so confirm first and say so.
- `POST /connections/{accountId}/disconnect` (`disconnectAccount`) works for **any** channel account, not only websites, and answers `connection` and `listings_ended`. The confirmation dialog should say:
  - its listings end;
  - its automatic writes stop;
  - its orders, stock history and VAT records are kept.

The module toggle (WEB-1, ADR-059): every website route carries `module: 'website_connector'` in the contract and in `openapi.json` (`x-permissions.module`). With `modules.website_connector` off, the server answers 404 on them all, so show no website entry at all, as your gating already does. Disconnect has no module.

Not for the UI: the inbound `/v1/website/...` routes are called by the website itself, signed.
